# Phase 6.7 — BugWiser: Sand Dominion

Scope: **BugWiser only.** ResQ, ENN Consultancy and Saravaraj AI were not redesigned.
Mission HQ v3, the transition architecture and identities, the reverse-scroll
correction, the four-entry Field Archive and the verified project facts were left
as they were.

## Why this pass replaced the first 6.7 attempt

The first 6.7 version read as a flat particle effect followed by a small square
screenshot deck. The sand came from only one edge, the introduction copy was
missing at desktop sizes, and the gallery was too small to inspect. This pass
rebuilds the chapter rather than tuning it:

- one full-viewport sand stage with two mirrored banks that gather, meet, split
  from the centre and sweep outward;
- the title and editorial introduction revealed through the opening;
- a large 16:10 floating deck of real application screens on desktop
  (390×844 screens on phones), with neighbours visible behind it in depth;
- a single open editorial record instead of a card/aside layout.

## Source truth and assets

- Facts, rank, stack and both URLs come from `lib/data/missions.ts` and the
  deployed application. Nothing from a plan or a mockup is presented as done.
- `https://bug-wiser.vercel.app/` was verified reachable and
  `https://github.com/PhenomDeekey27/BugWiser` is the verified repository.

  - the six-step investigation workflow (`screen-workflow`);
  - the investigation workspace with root-cause evidence and patch preview
    (`screen-investigation`).
- Desktop captures are 1440×900 at 2× (2880×1800). Phones are served a separate
  390×844 capture at 2× (780×1688) through `<picture>` and Next's documented
  `getImageProps` art-direction pattern, so the interface stays legible rather
  than being a shrunken desktop page.
- No screenshot, interface, logo or metric was fabricated. No existing asset was
  overwritten; the previous `bugwiser-*.png` files and `bugwiser-live.png`

- The sand banks use a new authored texture,
  `public/assets/missions/bugwiser/sand-wall.png` (760×1064, 48-colour palette,
  ~160 KB), generated by `scripts/build-sand-wall.py`. The right bank is the same
  texture mirrored with `scaleX(-1)`, so both formations read as one continuous
  sand wall. The older `sand-grain.png` remains the fine grain overlay.

## Direction

Four chapters, all full-bleed, none inside a portfolio card:

1. **Sand stage.** A `100svh` stage. Two banks enter from opposite edges, meet
   across the screen, hold, then split outward. Fine grains gather from both
   sides first and burst away after the split. A dark central scrim keeps the
   copy legible over the brightest sand.
2. **Editorial introduction.** Kicker, `BUGWISER`, a two-line statement of what
   the product does, and two short field notes (“The problem”, “Who it helps”).
   The title emerges through the opening in the banks rather than fading in over
   a static background.
3. **Floating application deck.** One large active screen, previous and next
   screens offset behind it, tilted in 3D, dimmed and lit by the gold
   environment. No frame or card around the group.
4. **Mission record.** A single editorial column/rail: What it is, Who it helps,
   Key features (the six implemented steps), Technology and access. Stack chips
   and two large functional link rows (repository, live product). Hairlines and
   whitespace instead of repeated feature cards.

## Sequence

All motion is CSS, armed by the existing one-shot `useEncounterActive` hook.
Base styles are the settled composition, so a failed observer or reduced motion
still shows a complete, readable chapter. The animation does not replay on
small scroll reversals; it runs once per chapter arming.

| Beat | Approx. time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera / currents | 0–0.6s | distant currents and haze settle |
| Gather | 0.25–1.7s | both banks enter from opposite edges, tilted; 14 grains pull in from both sides |
| Meet | ~1.6s | the two formations cover the viewport and meet at the centre seam |
| Split | ~1.8–3.65s | banks part from the centre and sweep outward; 16 grains burst away |
| Title | 2.25–3.65s | `BUGWISER` unmasks from the centre with a clip-path and tracking change |
| Introduction | 2.84–3.7s | role, statement and field notes rise into place |
| Settled | 3.4s+ | cue and caption arrive; sand clears; the stage holds still |

The stage does not lock scrolling, change scroll position or require a scroll
listener. It is one CSS timeline inside a normal document flow.

## Carousel behaviour

- Three real screens stay mounted. Changing index is a transform/opacity
  transition, not an image swap.
- Autoplay: **2,500 ms**.
- Transition: 820 ms transform, 620 ms opacity, 2,200 px perspective.
- Active slide at full size; neighbours at ±38% x-offset, −3% y, −180 px z,
  ±10° rotateY, 0.88 scale and 0.5 opacity. The 38% offset clears half the
  active frame, so the neighbours visibly peek behind it: 332–337 px per side
  at 1920, 234–237 px at 1366 and 15–25 px at 390. An earlier ±21% offset left
  them completely hidden behind the active screen.
- Pauses on hover, keyboard focus-visible inside the carousel, an active touch
  gesture, when the deck is out of view, and when the tab is hidden. The visible
  status changes from `Auto · 2.5s` to `Paused`; the dot stops its pulse.
- Manual previous/next buttons, three dot indicators, ArrowLeft/ArrowRight, and
  horizontal swipe on touch.
- Reduced motion: no autoplay, no perspective, no slide transition; the active
  screen is stable and every manual control still works.
- Non-active images are lazy-loaded, the deck reserves its aspect ratio
  (`1440 / 900` desktop, `390 / 844` mobile), and every screen has descriptive
  alt text naming what the interface shows.
- Deck size: `min(100%, 1180px, 112svh)` wide, measured 1,180×738 at 1920,
  860×538 at 1366, 390×844 at 430 and 350×757 at 390.

## Responsive behaviour

- The sand stage and banks scale down but remain full-viewport; on phones the
  banks are 86% wide and the central copy uses the full width with 18 px
  padding.
- The title is 168 px at 1920, 144.8 px at 1366 and 55.4 px at 390.
- The record collapses to one column at 1023 px; features become one column at
  900 px; access links become one column at 639 px.
- No horizontal overflow at 1920, 1366, 430, 390 or 375
  (`scrollWidth - innerWidth` between −10 and −1 px).

## Remaining limitations

- Only three genuine BugWiser application screens exist, so the deck has three
  slides. Nothing was recreated in HTML or invented to fill a fourth.
- The sand is a stylized CSS/SVG composition using an authored raster texture,
  not a physics simulation or a claimed character model.
- Browsers without CSS `animation-timeline` keep a static deck; there is no JS
  scroll-loop fallback.
- The sand sequence is one-shot per page load and does not replay on scroll-back,
  preserving the existing encounter-arming behaviour.
- The supplementary 375 and 430 responsive checks were captured before the final
  neighbour offset tuning; they verify deck sizing, lazy loading and overflow,
  not neighbour exposure.
